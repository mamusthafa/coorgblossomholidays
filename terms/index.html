Terms of Service & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Taxi Booking & Payment Terms

  • Zero Advance Needed: Most local sightseeing and airport transfer cabs require no advance deposit. You pay the driver directly at trip completion.
  • Package Pricing Inclusions: Quoted taxi rates include vehicle fuel and driver daily bata (allowance).
  • Exclusions: Government toll gates, state border entry permits (if applicable), and attraction parking fees are payable at actual entry counters.
  • Standard Operating Hours: Full-day sightseeing is typically scheduled between 8:30 AM and 7:00 PM. Night driving beyond 10:00 PM is subject to prior driver agreement.

2. Taxi Cancellation Policy

We understand travel plans can shift. If you need to cancel or postpone your cab booking, please inform us at least 4 to 6 hours before your scheduled pickup via phone or WhatsApp. There are no cancellation penalties for genuine advance notice.

3. Homestay Booking & House Rules

  • Direct Host Connection: Coorg Blossom Holidays assists travellers by connecting them directly with verified Kodagu estate and family homestay owners.
  • Check-in / Check-out: Standard check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM unless early check-in is pre-arranged with the host.
  • Identification: In accordance with local Kodagu tourism regulations, all adult guests must present valid government photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License) upon arrival.
  • Estate Respect: Guests are requested to respect plantation tranquility and avoid loud noise or outdoor music after 10:00 PM.
