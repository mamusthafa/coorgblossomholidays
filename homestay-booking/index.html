Arriving by Train? Direct Pickup from Mysore & Mangaluru to Your Homestay

Coorg has no railway connectivity of its own. Many coffee estate homestays and heritage bungalows are nestled deep in private plantations where outside autos or city drivers refuse to go or get lost. We arrange pre-booked platform exit pickup from Mysore Junction or Mangaluru Station and drop you safely right at your homestay portico.
