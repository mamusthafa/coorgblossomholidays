About Coorg Blossom Holidays

Based in Madikeri, Kodagu – dedicated to providing visitors with honest local taxi services and authentic homestay experiences across Coorg.

Who We Are

Coorg Blossom Holidays was founded with a singular commitment: to make traveling in the hill country of Kodagu simple, transparent, and enjoyable. We believe that tourists deserve genuine local drivers who know the ghat curves, historic sites, and hidden waterfalls like the back of their hand.

Unlike impersonal aggregators or third-party booking apps, you speak directly with our team in Madikeri. Whether you need a timely 4:00 AM airport pickup from Kannur or Mangalore, a 3-day family sightseeing circuit, or a peaceful cottage surrounded by Arabica coffee plants, we take personal responsibility for your holiday.
