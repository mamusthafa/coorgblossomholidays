Key Attractions Visited

Abbey Falls

Roaring cascade surrounded by coffee & black pepper plantations.

Raja's Seat

Panoramic western sunset viewpoint frequented by Kodagu kings.

Mandalpatti

Trekker peak and fog-covered ridges offering 360-degree views.

Dubare Elephant Camp

Bathing, feeding, and observing royal elephants in natural river settings.

Golden Temple Bylakuppe

Tibetan Buddhist spiritual center with 40-foot golden shrines.

Coffee Estates & Spice Walks

Walk through cardamom, vanilla, and Arabica coffee bushes.
