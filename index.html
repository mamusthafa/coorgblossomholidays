Near Madikeri Town (Within 2–5 km):

Best if you want easy access to local restaurants, shopping for spices/coffee/honey, ATMs, and attractions like Raja's Seat and Madikeri Fort without long drives.

🚆 Direct Railway Station Transfers

Mysore & Mangaluru Railway Station Pickup to Coorg

Coorg has no railway station of its own. Mysore Junction (120 km) and Mangaluru Central/Junction (140 km) are the two primary railheads. Our driver meets you right at the station exit with your name board and takes you directly to your homestay or hotel in Madikeri.
